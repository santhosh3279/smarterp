How billing works ​

The same flow your counter already follows — just faster.

Biller enters the bill

Pick the customer, scan or search items and save a draft. Stock is reserved as you save.

Cashier collects payment

Open the draft at the Cashier Desk, split the amount across payment modes and submit.

Print and dispatch

Print to the mapped printer, generate the E-Way Bill and record the loading receipt.

Books update themselves

Ledgers, GST, stock and incentives update instantly. Reports are ready at day end.
