# Features

SSPL Billing is a fast front-end for ERPNext, built for wholesale and retail counters. Every screen is grouped into a module below; your licence decides which modules each company can use, and user access decides who sees which screen.

<ModuleGrid />

## Highlights

### Sales & billing
- **Sales Entry** — keyboard-driven draft invoice entry with barcode scanning
- **Cashier Desk** — collect payment across multiple modes and submit
- **Single Entry** — one-shot billing for smaller counters
- **Sales Order & Quotation** — capture orders ahead of invoicing
- **Catalogue & Catalogue Viewer** — browse items with images and build orders
- **Cancellation & Modify Submitted Bill** — controlled corrections with automatic reversal of stock, ledger and incentives

### Purchase
- Purchase Invoice, Purchase Order and Purchase Desk for review and submit
- Price List Update for bulk rate changes
- Landed Cost Voucher to spread freight and duty onto received stock

### Inventory
- Store Transfer, Repack, Stock Reconciliation
- Barcode label printing
- Loading Receipt and Parcel Address for dispatch

### Accounts
- Customer Ledger and General Ledger with running balances
- Payment Receipt, Journal / Contra, Cash Box Entry
- Cheque Register and Unreconciled Entries

### GST & compliance
- GSTIN validation on Sales and Purchase Invoices
- GST Ledger for return filing
- E-Way Bill generation

### Real-time
- Bill panels and ledger balances refresh live across all open screens
- Items and customers cached in the browser for instant search
