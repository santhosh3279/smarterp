# Cashier desk

The **Cashier Desk** (Shift+F5) lists all draft bills waiting for payment, from every billing counter, updated live.

## Collecting payment

1. Select a draft from the list. The bill details and customer balance are shown.
2. Enter the amount received in each payment mode:
   - **Cash**
   - **UPI**
   - **Card**
   - **Discount** (if allowed)
   - **Credit** — the unpaid balance goes to the customer's account
3. The total of all modes must equal the bill amount.
4. Submit. The bill is finalised and ready to print.

::: warning Bill must be fully accounted
The desk does not allow submit until the whole bill amount is covered by payment modes or credit.
:::

## Outstanding bills

When a customer pays against old bills, use the outstanding bills option to allocate the payment to specific invoices.

## Cashier Management

**Cashier Management** handles the opening float, day-end cash count by denomination, and reconciliation of each cashier's collections against the system totals.

## Payment Receipt

To receive money not linked to a new bill — for example a customer settling old dues — use [Payment Receipt](./accounts#payment-receipt).
