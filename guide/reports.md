# Reports

Open **Reports** from the Dashboard for a list of all reports. Every report has date and store filters, and most can be exported to Excel.

## Sales

| Report | Shows |
|---|---|
| Daily Report | Sales and collections for the day |
| Store Sale Report | Sales totals per store |
| Store Wise Item Sales | Item sales split by store |
| Item Sales Summary | Total sales per item for a period |
| Cost Center Sale Report | Sales by cost center |
| Fast Moving Items | Best-selling items by quantity |

## Stock

| Report | Shows |
|---|---|
| Stock Status Report | Current stock on hand |
| Stock Aging Report | How long stock has been sitting, to find slow movers |
| Material Transfer Report | Stock moved between stores |
| Batch Reports | Stock and movement by batch |

## Accounts

| Report | Shows |
|---|---|
| Outstanding Customers Report | Customers with pending dues |
| Ledger Sales & Purchase | Combined sales and purchase ledger |
| Cashflow Report | Cash in and out over a period |
