# Incentives & loyalty

Reward customers or staff with incentive points based on what they buy or sell.

| Screen | Use it to |
|---|---|
| **Incentive Entry** | Record incentive earned on a transaction (press **Insert** while billing) |
| **Incentive Ledger** | See the running incentive balance per customer or employee |
| **Incentive Redeem** | Redeem accumulated incentive value |

Incentive rules can be a fixed rate or slab-based. When a bill is cancelled, the incentive earned on it is reversed automatically.
