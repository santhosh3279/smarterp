# Payments & ledgers

## Payment Receipt

**Payment Receipt** (Shift+F3) records money received from a customer or paid to a supplier.

1. Choose **Receive** or **Pay**.
2. Select the party.
3. Enter the amount and mode (cash, bank, UPI, cheque).
4. Optionally allocate against outstanding bills.
5. Save.

## Customer Ledger

**Customer Ledger** (Shift+F6) shows every transaction of a customer with a running balance. Click any line to see the voucher behind it. Balances refresh live when a new bill or payment is posted.

## General Ledger

Browse any account — cash, bank, expenses, income — with running balance and date filters.

## Journal / Contra

**Journal Contra** (Shift+F8) records manual adjustments and transfers between cash and bank accounts.

## Cash Box Entry

Record day-to-day shop expenses paid in cash.

## Cheque Register

Track cheques received and issued, with their clearing status.

## Unreconciled Entries & Payment Reconciliation

List payments not yet matched to invoices and link them, so that outstanding balances are accurate.

## Chart of Accounts

View the account tree and create new accounts where permitted.
