Pricing, discounts & offers ​

Price lists ​

Each billing series has its own default price list, so retail and wholesale counters can sell the same item at different rates.

Customer pricing ​

Set special rates for individual customers. These are applied automatically when that customer is billed.

Discount rules ​

Discount Rules let you set:

  • Slab discounts — for example 2% for 10–49 units, 5% for 50 units or more
  • Rules per item, item group or customer
  • Custom conditions where needed

Rules apply automatically while billing. ERPNext pricing rules can be synced into the same rule set.

Offer Display ​

Publish current offers and schemes to a customer-facing page — for example a screen at the shop entrance or a link shared on WhatsApp.
