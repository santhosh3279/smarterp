# Inventory & stock

Stock is updated automatically by sales and purchase bills. The screens below handle everything else.

| Screen | Use it to |
|---|---|
| **Store Transfer** (Shift+F9) | Move stock between stores or warehouses |
| **Repack Entry** | Convert bulk stock into smaller packs, or raw items into finished goods |
| **Stock Reconciliation** | Correct system stock to match a physical count |
| **Stock Check** | Look up stock and selling price quickly, including on a phone |
| **Landed Cost Voucher** | Add freight and other charges to stock value |
| **Print Barcodes** | Print barcode labels for items |
| **Loading Receipt** | Record goods loaded onto a vehicle for dispatch |
| **Parcel Address** | Keep delivery addresses for parcels and print labels |

## Physical stock count

1. Count the stock in the store.
2. Open **Stock Reconciliation** and select the warehouse.
3. Enter the counted quantity for each item. The difference is shown.
4. Submit. Stock and stock value are adjusted.

## Printing barcodes

1. Open **Print Barcodes**.
2. Add items and the number of labels for each.
3. Print to the label printer.
