# Purchase

## Purchase Invoice

1. Open **Purchase Invoice**.
2. Select the supplier (create a new one inline if needed — GSTIN and pincode are checked).
3. Enter the supplier's bill number and date.
4. Add items with quantity and rate. New items can be created from the same screen, including a photo.
5. Save. The invoice is staged for review.

## Purchase Desk

**Purchase Desk** (Shift+F4) lists staged purchase invoices. A supervisor checks them and submits — stock and supplier ledger are updated on submit.

## Purchase Order

**Purchase Order** (Shift+F7) records what you ordered from a supplier. Receive it later as a purchase invoice.

## Price List Update

Update selling or buying rates for many items at once — useful after a supplier price change.

## Landed Cost Voucher

Spread freight, loading and other charges across the items of a purchase so that stock value reflects the true cost.
