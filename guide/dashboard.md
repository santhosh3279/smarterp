# Dashboard & navigation

The Dashboard is the home screen after login. It shows a tile for every screen you are allowed to open, grouped into buckets.

## Buckets

| Bucket | Contains |
|---|---|
| **Sales** | Sales Invoice, Quotation, Sales Order, Cashier Desk, Cashier Management, Cancellation, Modify Submitted |
| **Purchase** | Purchase Invoice, Purchase Order, Purchase Desk |
| **Stock** | Stock Reconciliation, Store Transfer, Repack, Landed Cost Voucher, Stock Check |
| **Accounts** | Cash Box Entry, Single Entry, Payment Receipt, Unreconciled Entries, Payment Reconciliation, Journal Contra |
| **Ledger View** | Customer Ledger, General Ledger, GST Ledger |
| **SSPL Special** | Catalogue, Offer Display, Incentives, Barcode Print, Loading Receipt, Parcel Address |
| **Report** | Daily Report, Store Sales, Stock Status, Stock Aging, Outstanding Customers and more |

A tile you don't see is either not in your company's licence or not granted to your user.

## Opening a screen

- Click the tile, or
- Press the tile's shortcut key (shown on the tile), or
- Press **F1** to open the Command Line and type the screen name.

Press **Esc** on most screens to go back to the Dashboard.

## Dashboard shortcuts

| Key | Opens |
|---|---|
| Shift+F3 | Payment Receipt |
| Shift+F4 | Purchase Desk |
| Shift+F5 | Cashier Desk |
| Shift+F6 | Customer Ledger |
| Shift+F7 | Purchase Order |
| Shift+F8 | Journal Contra |
| Shift+F9 | Store Transfer |
| Shift+F10 | Quotation |

## Theme

Use the theme toggle in the header to switch between light and dark mode. The choice is remembered on that computer.
