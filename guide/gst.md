# GST & compliance

## GSTIN validation

When a customer or supplier is created, or an invoice is saved, the GSTIN is checked and the GST category set automatically.

## GST Ledger

The **GST Ledger** lists taxable transactions grouped for return filing.

## E-Way Bill

Generate an E-Way Bill from a submitted invoice for goods movement. Vehicle and transporter details are entered in the E-Way Bill window.

## WGB Payments

Supports reconciliation of GST payments.
